Konfigurasi pin iMCLab (DIPASTIKAN dari program dosen, folder UTS/referensi_dosen):
  IN1 = GPIO 27, IN2 = GPIO 26, EN (PWM) = GPIO 12, PWM 30 kHz 8-bit
  Arah maju     : IN1 HIGH, IN2 LOW
  ENCODER       = GPIO 13, INPUT_PULLUP, interrupt FALLING, debounce 500 us
  Lubang piringan = 2 per putaran
  LED           = GPIO 2
  USB-serial    = Silicon Labs CP210x
Catatan: GPIO 12 adalah strapping pin ESP32 (menentukan tegangan flash saat boot).
Jika board gagal boot/upload, coba upload dengan adaptor DC dicabut.
